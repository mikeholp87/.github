# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/): be respectful, welcoming and constructive; no harassment or discrimination; assume good faith.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that violate this, and may block contributors who repeatedly do. To report a concern, use GitHub's private contact options on the repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability", or a direct message to the maintainer).
